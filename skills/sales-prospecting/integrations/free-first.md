# Free-first email discovery choices

**Snapshot: 2026-11-06. Recheck current official plan, API access, credits,
verification accuracy, geography, terms, and data-processing conditions.**

| Provider | Published free allowance | Native integration / caveat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hunter | 50 credits/month | Official MCP and API on Free; verified email finder, domain search and verifier (operations have different credit costs). |
| Prospeo | 100 credits/month | 1 credit per successful verified email reveal; limited free API, no full free bulk processing. |
| Apollo | 900 credits/year granted monthly | Broad prospect data. Confirm credit cost for the requested export/enrichment and current plan entitlements. |
| Lusha | 40 credits/month | Official ChatGPT and Claude connectors; one verified email reveal generally costs 1 credit; CRM exports can cost separately. |
| Snov.io | Free plan with 50 email-finding credits | Free API/integrations and higher-volume operations have restrictions; exact renewal terms need checking. |

For very small batches, start with public business websites; use an enrichment
provider only for the contacts that genuinely match the product. A verified
company role and address matter more than a large result count.

Provider home/pricing/docs:
- https://hunter.io/pricing and https://help.hunter.io/en/articles/1970956-hunter-api
- https://help.prospeo.io/en/article/how-to-create-an-account-on-prospeo-for-free-5inxir/
- https://www.apollo.io/pricing
- https://www.lusha.com/pricing/
- https://snov.io/knowledgebase/faq-pricing-plans/

**CRM and email:** HubSpot Free can work with a Gmail extension for email
logging. Zoho CRM offers a free CRM with plan-specific feature limits; paid
plans may be required for direct mailbox integration. Do not claim a paid
Salesforce plan is free. Native mailbox + CSV remains valid.
