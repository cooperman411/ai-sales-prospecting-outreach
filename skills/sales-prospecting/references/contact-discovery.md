# Contact discovery: public and free-first

1. Search official company pages, public staff directories, and current roles.
   Respect access controls and site terms. A public generic business address
   is not proof it reaches the relevant buyer.
2. Check the current CRM/suppression list before buying data or creating leads.
3. Prefer a verified professional work address; never infer a personal email,
   privately scrape profiles, or fabricate name-pattern guesses as deliverable.
4. When the contact is not public, propose an approved provider lookup. Obtain
   a clear budget and credit ceiling before spending or revealing personal
   contact information. Record provider, source date, email-verification
   status, and any limits. 'Valid syntax' is not delivery verification.
5. Compare provider results against official/current company and role evidence.
   Do not overwrite a conflicting CRM field without permission.
6. Excluded, opted-out, unsupported jurisdiction, hard bounce, unknown role,
   or unverified mailbox: do not send.

**Free-first options:** Hunter (official native MCP/API), Prospeo (100 free
credits/month with constrained API), Apollo (free tier with credit allotment),
Lusha (native connectors, smaller free quota), Snov.io (trial/free allowance).
See `integrations/free-first.md`; check live terms each time.

Do not equate provider credits with a guaranteed number of usable contacts.
Retain only data necessary for the stated outreach purpose, privately, and
apply applicable privacy, retention and access restrictions.
