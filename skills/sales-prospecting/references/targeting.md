# Evidence-based targeting

Translate a user-provided ICP into visible hard requirements and softer signals:
industry, use case, company characteristics, geography, relevant functions,
verified trigger, disqualifiers, and number of companies to investigate.
Do not introduce arbitrary filters as facts. Separate:

- **Verified:** what an official company or other credible source states.
- **Inferred:** plausible fit with explicit caveat.
- **Unknown:** missing evidence; not a negative finding.
- **Excluded:** actual failure of a hard filter or user suppression.

Start with a small sample, review fit with the user, and refine before scaling.
Prefer a few credible contacts at a relevant company to a large unqualified
list. Return direct primary-source URLs, a last-checked date, and coverage
boundary. Do not claim 'all' businesses were found from a bounded query.
