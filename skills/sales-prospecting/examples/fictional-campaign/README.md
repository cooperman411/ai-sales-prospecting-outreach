# Fictional Campaign

This example shows how to record a company, a possible buyer role, and an unsent draft. The files use a reserved example domain and leave the work address blank.

Imagine you help field service teams schedule jobs. A company's fictional operations update says it is adding a second service area. That gives you a reason to ask whether its team would find a dispatch checklist useful.

> **Subject:** Question about your public operations update
>
> I saw in your operations update that you're adding a second service area this spring. I help field service teams plan their dispatch schedules. Would a one-page checklist for assigning jobs across two areas be useful?

The example stops at a draft because the recipient is unresolved. In a real campaign, verify the person and work address, check eligibility, and add the approved sender details and opt-out information before sending.
